THE KIRKLAND MARKET

Buying a Home in Kirkland, WA

Kirkland combines walkable Lake Washington waterfront living with one of the Eastside's strongest employment hubs — anchored by Google's Kirkland Urban & 6th Street campuses. With a median home price near $1.4 million, a traditional 2.5% buyer commission costs roughly $35,000.

Families seek out the Lake Washington School District across Houghton, Moss Bay, Rose Hill, Juanita, Finn Hill, and Totem Lake. Because Kirkland has limited lakefront inventory and strong demand from Bellevue and Redmond commuters, well-structured offers move quickly.

With Fair Fee Buyer, you pay one flat $2,999 fee — covering up to 3 NWMLS Form 21 offers, inspection and sewer-scope contingencies, HOA/condo document review, counter-offer strategy, and closing coordination.

98033 vs. 98034 Kirkland Neighborhoods

98033 (Moss Bay, Houghton, Market, Rose Hill, Bridle Trails) commands premium pricing close to Downtown Kirkland, Lake Washington parks, and Google. 98034 (Juanita, Finn Hill, Kingsgate, Totem Lake) offers wider options from lake-access condos to family homes near The Village at Totem Lake.

Low Commission vs. Flat Fee in Kirkland

Many Kirkland buyers search for a low-commission buyer's agent or rebate broker. Instead of a percentage fee or complex rebate tiers, our $2,999 flat fee is fixed from day one in your RCW 18.86 Buyer Brokerage Agreement — whether you buy an $850K condo or a $2.2M waterfront home.

Kirkland Inspections, Condos & Waterfront Rules

Older Juanita and Rose Hill homes often benefit from sewer scope and crawlspace inspections, while downtown condos require careful review of the HOA resale certificate and reserve study. We make sure every contingency deadline is protected through closing.
