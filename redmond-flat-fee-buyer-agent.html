THE REDMOND MARKET

Buying a Home in Redmond, WA

Redmond is one of the Eastside's most competitive tech-driven housing markets — home to Microsoft's global headquarters and Nintendo of America. Median prices sit near $1.2M in 98052 and $1.39M in 98053, and move-in-ready homes near top schools or light rail still attract fast competition.

Families target the Lake Washington School District (note that a small pocket of southeast Redmond falls in Bellevue School District), while the 2 Line light rail at Downtown Redmond and Overlake Village has reshaped commuting to Bellevue and Seattle.

On a $1.3M Redmond purchase, a traditional 2.5% buyer's agent fee equals $32,500. With Fair Fee Buyer, you pay one transparent $2,999 flat fee for up to 3 NWMLS Form 21 offers, escalation and contingency strategy, inspection negotiation, and closing support — keeping nearly $30,000 for your down payment or closing costs.

98052 vs. 98053 Redmond Neighborhoods

98052 covers Downtown Redmond, Grass Lawn, Overlake, and Idylwood — shorter commutes to Microsoft and light rail, with a mix of townhomes, condos, and mid-century single-family homes. 98053 (Education Hill north & Bear Creek) offers larger lots and newer subdivisions at a higher median price.

How Flat Fee Works With Seller Compensation

Under Washington's RCW 18.86 and the post-2024 NAR rules, we sign a transparent Buyer Brokerage Agreement specifying the $2,999 flat fee before submitting offers. If a Redmond seller offers buyer-broker compensation or concessions, we structure your offer so your net price stays as strong as possible.

Winning Competitive Redmond Offers

Listing agents in Education Hill and Overlake care about clean financing, realistic closing dates, and well-drafted escalation clauses (NWMLS Form 35E) — not whether your agent pockets 2.5%. Lowering brokerage overhead can even make your offer more attractive to the seller's net sheet.
